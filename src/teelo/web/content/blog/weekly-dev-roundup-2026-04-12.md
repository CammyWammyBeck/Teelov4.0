# Weekly Update — Teelo Can Now Forecast Entire Tournaments

I missed last week’s roundup, so this one is covering everything since the last post.

The big change is a fun one: Teelo can now forecast entire tournaments, not just individual matches.

Up until now, Teelo has mostly answered one question: who is likely to win this match?

Now it can start answering a much better one as well: what does this whole tournament actually look like?

That means once a draw is live, you can look beyond the next match and get a feel for the whole event — who is most likely to make the quarter-finals, semis, final, and who is the most likely winner overall.

## Tournament forecasts are live

This is probably the biggest product step forward since the last roundup.

Match predictions are still the heart of Teelo, but tournament forecasts add a whole new layer on top. Instead of seeing each match in isolation, you can now follow the shape of the draw as a whole.

I think that makes Teelo a lot more useful.

A lot of the time, the interesting part of a tournament is not just who wins the next match. It is seeing which quarter looks brutal, where the path has opened up, who has quietly become a real chance of making a run, and which favourite might be less safe than they look.

That is exactly the kind of thing this feature is meant to make easier.

I have also added the forecast view across the site so it actually feels built in, rather than like a side page you would never find unless someone linked you to it.

## It gives Teelo a much broader view of an event

This is the part I like most.

If you are following a tournament, you usually care about more than one match at a time. You want to know how the bracket is developing. You want to know who is looming in the next round, who landed in the roughest section, and whether a draw is opening up for someone unexpectedly.

That is where tournament forecasts help.

It turns Teelo into more of a tournament-tracking tool, rather than just a next-match predictor. That broader view has been missing for a while, so it is nice to finally have it live.

## There were a few smaller improvements too

Most of the work went into getting the forecast feature live and making it feel properly integrated, but there were a few other useful improvements as well.

The matches page is cleaner to use now, especially when switching between completed matches and upcoming ones. I also fixed an annoying Challenger filter issue, so browsing results and fixtures should feel a bit smoother in general.

Those are smaller updates, but they help the site feel nicer to use day to day.

## Summary

So the short version is: since the last roundup, Teelo has gone from predicting individual matches to also forecasting entire tournaments.

That is a pretty meaningful jump.

You can still use it the same way as before if you just want match predictions, but now there is also a much better sense of the bigger picture — how a draw is shaping up, who the live contenders are, and where the real pressure points sit.

Still plenty more to build, but this feels like a proper step forward. Teelo is starting to feel less like a page of isolated predictions, and more like something that helps you understand the whole tournament.
